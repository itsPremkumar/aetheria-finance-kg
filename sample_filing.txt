PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Overview

Acme Corp Inc. ("Acme," the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a leading global technology company headquartered in New York, NY. Founded in 2010, we provide innovative software solutions to enterprise customers worldwide. Our common stock trades on NASDAQ under the ticker symbol ACME.

Acquisitions

On January 15, 2024, Acme Corp Inc. acquired WidgetCo Ltd. for $500 million in cash and stock. WidgetCo Ltd. is a cloud-based analytics platform that enhances our data visualization capabilities.

On March 3, 2024, Acme Corp Inc. acquired DataFlow Systems Corp. for $750 million. DataFlow Systems Corp. provides real-time data pipeline solutions.

Investments

During the quarter ended March 31, 2024, Acme Corp Inc. invested in StartupXYZ Corp. $100 million as part of a Series B funding round. StartupXYZ Corp. is an AI-powered customer service platform.

On June 15, 2024, Acme Corp Inc. invested in GreenEnergy Solutions Inc. $250 million to support our sustainability initiatives.

Strategic Partnerships

On September 1, 2024, Acme Corp Inc. entered into a strategic partnership with GlobalTech Industries to co-develop next-generation cloud infrastructure solutions.

Competition

Our primary competitors include MegaCorp Industries, SuperCo Inc., and TechGiant Corp. We compete on the basis of product innovation, customer service, and price.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Our business faces numerous risks and uncertainties, including:

- We operate in a highly competitive market with low barriers to entry for certain product segments.
- Our acquisitions may not achieve expected synergies. The acquisitions of WidgetCo Ltd. and DataFlow Systems Corp. involve integration risks.
- Our investments in StartupXYZ Corp. and GreenEnergy Solutions Inc. are subject to market volatility and may not generate expected returns.
- Changes in regulatory environments could adversely affect our operations.
- Our international operations expose us to currency fluctuations and geopolitical risks.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Overview

For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024, we reported revenue of $12.5 billion, an increase of 15% from the prior year. Net income was $2.1 billion, or $4.20 per diluted share.

Results of Operations

Revenue: Revenue increased $1.6 billion, or 15%, to $12.5 billion in 2024 from $10.9 billion in 2023. This growth was driven by increased demand for our cloud-based solutions and contributions from recent acquisitions.

Cost of Revenue: Cost of revenue increased $800 million, or 12%, to $7.5 billion in 2024 from $6.7 billion in 2023.

Gross Profit: Gross profit increased $800 million, or 19%, to $5.0 billion in 2024 from $4.2 billion in 2023.

Operating Expenses: Operating expenses increased $500 million, or 16%, to $3.6 billion in 2024 from $3.1 billion in 2023.

Net Income: Net income increased $300 million, or 17%, to $2.1 billion in 2024 from $1.8 billion in 2023.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of December 31, 2024, we had $5.0 billion in cash and cash equivalents. We believe our existing cash and cash equivalents, together with cash generated from operations, will be sufficient to meet our working capital and capital expenditure needs for at least the next 12 months.
